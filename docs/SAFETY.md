# Medical and data safety requirements

- The AI is not a doctor and must not claim to diagnose or replace clinical care.
- Do not generate prescriptions, antibiotic regimens or medication dosages.
- Ask brief clarifying questions when symptoms are insufficient.
- Escalate possible emergencies immediately; do not keep the user chatting.
- Emergency keyword matching is only a basic safety net and can miss emergencies. It is not a validated clinical triage system.
- Do not send personally identifying or sensitive patient data to the model unless the privacy/legal basis and vendor terms have been reviewed.
- Before real deployment, obtain qualified clinician review, security review, privacy/legal review, and appropriate regulatory advice for Bangladesh and any target country.
